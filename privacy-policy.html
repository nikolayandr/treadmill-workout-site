Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Treadmill Workout ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Profile data: body weight and height, entered voluntarily for calorie calculations. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Workout data: workout configurations, segment settings, and workout history. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Account information: if you choose to sign in, we collect your email address to enable data synchronization across devices.

Information collected automatically:

  • Advertising data: our free version displays ads through Google AdMob, which may collect advertising identifiers, device information, and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase data: in-app purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. Purchase status is used solely to determine your subscription tier.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide workout tracking, calorie estimation, and training features
  • To synchronize your data across devices when you sign in
  • To manage your subscription status (Trial, Free, or Pro)
  • To display advertisements in the free version

3. Data Storage and Security

Your profile and workout data are stored locally on your device by default. If you sign in with an account, your data may be synchronized to our servers for cross-device access. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit — for processing in-app purchases
  • Firebase — for authentication and data synchronization (if applicable)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With advertising partners (Google AdMob) as described above, in the free version only
  • When required by law or legal process

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads: you can adjust your ad preferences in your device settings
  • Delete your data: you can delete your profile and workout history from within the app settings. If you have an account, you can request full account deletion by contacting us
  • Remove ads: upgrade to Pro to remove all advertisements

7. Children's Privacy

Treadmill Workout is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
