Delete Your Account

If you would like to delete your Treadmill Workout account and all associated data, you can do so using one of the methods below.

Option 1: From within the app

Open Treadmill Workout → tap your profile icon → Account Settings → Delete Account. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted.

Option 2: By email

Send an email to nikolay.androsovich@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account" and include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 business days.

What data will be deleted

  • Your account profile (email, name)
  • Body metrics (weight, height)
  • Saved workout configurations
  • Workout history
  • Subscription status (active subscriptions will be cancelled)

Note: Data stored only on your device (if you never signed in) is not on our servers. You can clear it by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings.

Note: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription through Google Play or the App Store. Please cancel your subscription in your device's subscription settings before requesting deletion.
